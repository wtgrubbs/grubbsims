ModZero: Simulation Rules

  • Team Dynamics: Worms of the same team safely phase through one another and themselves.
  • Prime Armor: Prime numbers act as absolute armor. If the head of a worm collides with an opponent's prime segment, they simply pass through each other.
  • Prime Harvesting: Colliding with an opponent's nonprime (composite) segment triggers a harvest from the opponent's tail.
  • Death by Consumption: If a worm's final prime (its head) is stolen, it dies and is removed.
  • Death by Starvation: If a worm is reduced to only its head prime (length of 1), it has a 10-cycle window to successfully harvest a prime from an opponent. If it fails, it starves and dies.
  • Total Wipeout: If a team is reduced to zero living worms, the game immediately ends and the remaining team is victorious.

Strategy & Advice

The Composite Buffer: Having long strings of nonprime composite numbers makes a worm physically longer, which increases its speed. However, they are highly vulnerable points of attack.

Evolution & Saving: At the end of a match, you can save your starting species to a CSV file. If your species wins, its "Victory Count" increases, allowing you to track the pedigree of your finest creations over time!
