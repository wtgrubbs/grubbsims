Underlying Theory & Functionality

Purpose: This application bridges the qualitative understanding of MO theory with the rigorous linear algebra methods of the Hückel approach.

Workflow & Features:

  • Matrix Building: Construct a molecular topology directly via the adjacency matrix. The system automatically enforces symmetry (if atom 1 is bonded to 2, atom 2 is bonded to 1) and validates maximum 3-coordination for planar realism.
  • Math & Secular Determinants: Generates the |H - ES| = 0 determinant, assuming standard Hückel approximations (α for diagonal, β for adjacent neighbors, S=I). Evaluates the characteristic polynomial using the Faddeev-LeVerrier algorithm.
  • Jacobi Diagonalization: Iteratively calculates eigenvalues (energy states: E = α + xβ) and eigenvectors (LCAO coefficients).
  • Degeneracies & Hund's Rule: The energy diagram strictly aligns degenerate states (difference < 0.001) horizontally. Electrons follow the Aufbau principle, filling the lowest energy first, and Hund's Rule, maximizing spin multiplicity for degenerate sets by placing one electron in each orbital before pairing.
  • 3D Wavefunction Projection: Clicking an MO maps its eigenvector to the 3D topology. Node size reflects amplitude (|c| scaled), and color indicates phase sign (Red = positive phase, Blue = negative phase), visualizing nodal planes.
