Lewis Structure Reference Guide

1. Counting Valence Electrons

Sum the valence electrons for all atoms in the molecule based on their periodic table group:

  • Hydrogen (H): 1 e⁻
  • Carbon (C): 4 e⁻
  • Nitrogen (N): 5 e⁻
  • Oxygen (O): 6 e⁻
  • Halogens (F, Cl, Br, I): 7 e⁻

2. Calculating Formal Charge

FC = (Valence e⁻) − (Nonbonding e⁻) − (Bonding e⁻ / 2)

3. Valid vs. Optimal Structures

This application allows you to construct any valid Lewis structure. The best Lewis structures distribute electrons in a way that minimizes formal charges across all atoms.
